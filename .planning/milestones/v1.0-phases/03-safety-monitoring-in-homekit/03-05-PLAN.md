<objective>
Fill out the catalogue the tracer established: the water services and their flood adapter, both pump
services and both live activity adapters, the battery pair, and the four remaining Apple Home fault
adapters with their owning-service status.

Purpose: the tracer proved the architecture on one field. Everything here is expansion out from it —
the same row shape, the same projection contract, the same get-or-add and `StatusActive` discipline,
applied to the remaining eleven services. Doing it as one table with one small projection per row is
a correctness constraint rather than a preference: `fallow dupes` runs with `--fail-on-issues` at a
zero baseline, and eleven near-identical inline publish blocks would be a clone family that fails
`npm run check`.
Output: every service this phase publishes, with its exact vendor facts read-only beside its standard
characteristics.
</objective>
