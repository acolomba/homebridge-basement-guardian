<objective>
Give silence a forward-only base, and make the anchor a clamp that can only ever report silence
sooner.

Purpose: silence is measured against a jumpable wall clock today, so a backwards jump larger than the
window leaves a dead pump reading as trustworthy — the failure IN-03 exists to close. A separate
`MonotonicClock` port fixes the base, and one `max` expression covers a backwards jump (the monotonic
term wins), a system suspend (the wall term wins) and, once plan 05.1-05 persists the anchor, a
restart (only the wall term exists).

Output: the port, its production instance, the clamp, a movable monotonic clock beside the movable
wall clock the unit suite already has, and a Cucumber scenario in which the system clock moves
backwards and the tile does not go back to vouching.
</objective>
