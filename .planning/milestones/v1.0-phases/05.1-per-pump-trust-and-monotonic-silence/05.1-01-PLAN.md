<objective>
Fix the two harness defects the ledger records as entries 17 and 18 **before** the harness carries
this phase's per-pump evidence (D-12). Both ledger entries state their mechanism wrongly; research
corrected both, and this plan builds the corrected diagnosis rather than the ledger text.

Purpose: entry 18's real defect is a wait that returns instantly on any reconnect, because
`publishedTopics` is cumulative and never reset. A per-pump silence scenario naturally drives
reconnects and staggered heartbeats, so a scenario built on this harness could pass for exactly the
wrong reason on exactly the behaviour this phase adds. Entry 17's real defect is a second
`currentAccessory` that shadows the shared, documented one and reads the *first* accessory of the
*first* registration call — which on a two-device account is whichever pump registered first.

Output: a per-connection, per-device subscription wait; a `Then` step that pins it; one shared
`currentAccessory` rule; and a `topicNamed` that takes its device rather than closing over one.
</objective>
