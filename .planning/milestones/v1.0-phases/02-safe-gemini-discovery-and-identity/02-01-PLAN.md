<objective>
Prove the phase's riskiest new integration — vendor payload validation through a family registry
into a real Homebridge `PlatformAccessory` with a populated `AccessoryInformation` service — as one
thin, end-to-end, production-quality slice, then complete Gemini's field-level validation and
decoding behind it.

Purpose: every later plan in this phase (family three-way outcome, identity unification, removal,
rename, degrade-in-place) extends the registry/accessory/platform wiring this plan establishes. A
mistake in the HAP `AccessoryInformation` handling, the registry shape, or the UUID-seeding
discipline is far cheaper to catch here, on one device, than after five more plans have built on it.
Output: `src/device/registry.ts` (new), `src/device/gemini.ts` upgraded to a real `DeviceFamily`
implementation, `src/accessories/basementGuardian.ts` upgraded to a real accessory factory,
`src/platform.ts` wired to register one newly discovered Gemini, and a fake-HAP test harness capable
of proving all of it through a Cucumber scenario.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| Vendor REST device snapshot -> family validation | Untrusted wire-shaped telemetry/metadata crossing into `DeviceFamily.validate()`/`decode()`. |
| Decoded domain state -> `accessory.context` / log lines | Any value that could carry more of the raw vendor record than the plugin intends to expose. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|------------------|
| T-02-02 | Tampering | `src/device/gemini.ts` validate/decode ordering, `src/accessories/basementGuardian.ts` | high | mitigate | `decode()` is called only after `validate(snapshot).valid === true`; enforced by `basementGuardian.ts`'s `update()` and covered by the "decode never runs after a failed validate" test in Task 2. |
| T-02-09 | Information Disclosure | `src/device/gemini.ts` decode(), `src/accessories/basementGuardian.ts` update() | low | mitigate | `decode()` and `AccessoryInformation` population build every field explicitly (never `{ ...snapshot.metadata }` or `{ ...snapshot.data }`), matching the `toApiDevice` field-by-field discipline in `src/cloud/types.ts` and the sanitized-artifact discipline of D-027, so an unread vendor key can never reach domain state, context, or a log line. |

</threat_model>
