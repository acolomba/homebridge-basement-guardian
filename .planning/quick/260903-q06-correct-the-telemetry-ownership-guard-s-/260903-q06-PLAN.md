<the_governing_rule>

**The guard stays. Its reason changes. Its behaviour does not.**

`nextShadowVersion` keeps reading `patch.data`. If a step of this task would alter what any function
does, that step is out of scope and the deviation goes in the summary instead.

**Write the reason that is reachable, not the one that reads well.** `patch.data` is set by
`toReportedPatch` (`src/cloud/shadow.ts:190`) as `isRecord(sections.data) ? sections.data : undefined`.
The only validation above it is `isShadowDocument` (`:162`), which answers
`isRecord(value) && isRecord(value.state)`. That is two levels: the payload is an object, and its
`state` is an object. It says nothing about `reported` or about the sections below it. So
`patch.data === undefined` is reachable from four inputs:

1. `data` absent -- the vendor shape the probe looked for and did not find.
2. `data` present and `null` -- `isRecord(null)` is false.
3. `data` present and a string, a number or an array -- the same.
4. `reported` missing or not a record -- `sections` falls back to `{}`.

Inputs 2 and 3 are a truncated payload, wire corruption, or a vendor bug. They parse, they pass
validation, and they reach the guard intact. Remove the guard and such a document takes ownership of
readings it never delivered, locking the poll out for about 30 minutes with a stale value on the
tile. That is CR-03's false normal, arriving through corruption rather than through shape.

**Do not overstate the probe.** It watched one device in steady state for 90 minutes and saw six
heartbeats. No pump cycle, fault, power event, reconnect or firmware update occurred, so event-driven
reports are unmeasured. Every sentence this task writes about the probe states its sample size and
its limits.

**Do not plan the change the maintainer did not choose.** Arming the silence timer on telemetry
rather than on any message is an available option. It is recorded as an option in the ledger. It is
not made here.
