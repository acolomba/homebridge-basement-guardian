Connect the record the previous plan can compute to the characteristics a user actually reads, and to
the disk it has to survive on.

accessory drives one and the composition root supplies the other, nothing about `CTRL-01` is true for
a real installation. This is the plan where an owner can first restart Homebridge and see the same
count come back.
Output: both Pump services carrying their record, and the record surviving a restart.
