<objective>
Drive every way a control request can end, and a full restart of the record, through the real plugin
against the fake vendor cloud — the layer where a defect looks like the defect a user would meet.

Purpose: the unit tests prove each rule in isolation against stand-ins the phase wrote. These
scenarios prove the rules survive contact with the real accessory, the real catalogue, the real
command port, the real REST client, and the real shadow path. They are also where the fake vendor
service learns to arm a command outcome without accidentally capturing a poll, and where the fake pump
learns to react to a command it accepted.
Output: six control scenarios, one record-survival scenario, and a fake that can drive all of them
without a sleep.
</objective>
