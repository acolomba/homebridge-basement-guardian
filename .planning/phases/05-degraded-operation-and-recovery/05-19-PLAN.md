<objective>
Make the two documents an owner reads say what the plugin does, and close the round's records against
what was actually run.

Purpose: `05-REVIEW-2.md` CR-04 and the documentation half of WR-08. `README.md` promises that the
delay a quiet live connection causes is "to the report, never to a reading", and that a poll finding a
flooded pit reports it. That is false for the whole window the paragraph is about: the readings stay
with the live path until it has missed two heartbeats, about thirty minutes, and the phase ratified
that and tests it deliberately -- there is a named unit case pinning the poll losing inside that
window. The paragraph even admits the marking is late while asserting the reading is not, which is the
inverse of what the code does. An owner reading it concludes a flooded pit will always show.
`CHANGELOG.md` carries the same claim in one clause. A second, smaller overstatement says every
service stops answering under a credential refusal; the pass reaches every service that carries a
trust report, and the next sentence in the README already says so.

Output: two corrected sections, a corrected changelog entry, reconciled requirement rows, and the
round's validation records completed.
</objective>
