<objective>
Wire `reconciliation.ts` (built standalone in `02-02`) into the account runtime's poll cycle, add the
out-of-band final-check fetch `D-029` requires before a removal commits, and connect confirmed
removal through to Homebridge's `unregisterPlatformAccessories` in `platform.ts`.

Purpose: this is the last piece of `DEV-05` — the counting state machine already exists and is fully
unit-tested; what remains is the specific two-step commit protocol (`confirmedAbsent` from the normal
poll, then one independent re-fetch immediately before acting) that closes the race `02-CONTEXT.md`
names: a device reappearing in the interval between the second confirming poll and the removal
decision. This plan also updates `platform.ts`'s class-level "the platform removes nothing" doc
comment and its Phase-1 test, since `DEV-05` is the first requirement to make that no longer true.
Output: `accountRuntime.ts` performs the two-confirmation-plus-final-check protocol and reports
confirmed removals; `platform.ts` acts on them.
</objective>

      nothing as a device-offline condition (a monitoring-path failure, not a device fact, matching the
      existing `recordPollFailure` distinction), and leaves the pending deviceIds to be re-evaluated on
      the next successful poll's own `confirmedAbsent` computation (no separate retry state).
