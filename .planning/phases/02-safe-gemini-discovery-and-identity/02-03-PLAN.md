<objective>
Complete the family registry's three-way outcome (`DEV-01`) — implemented Gemini, unsupported HALO,
unknown `deviceTypeId` — and wire `platform.ts`'s discovery handler to dispatch every discovered
device through it, so a HALO or unknown-profile device produces a distinct, once-per-run log
explanation and never blocks a valid Gemini in the same inventory. Gemini-only v1 with HALO explained
and skipped is D-002; this plan is where that decision becomes runtime behavior.

Purpose: Plan 02-01's tracer only ever saw one Gemini device; this plan proves the registry's core
promise — that adding a second, third, or unsupported family is a data change to a `Map`, never a
change to `platform.ts` or the account runtime (`DEV-02`'s "future families addable without changing
account-wide infrastructure" is an architectural property verified by construction here, not a
discrete edge case: the registry stays a `Map` populated at module load, and this plan's tests prove
a HALO/unknown entry never reaches decode without adding a single conditional to the dispatch loop).
Output: `src/device/registry.ts` with all three outcomes and log-cadence tracking; `src/device/halo.ts`
gains a runtime-usable identity constant; `platform.ts` dispatches every discovered device through
the registry regardless of outcome.
</objective>
