<objective>
Make `createBasementGuardianAccessory` re-resolve the family registry and re-validate on every
`update(snapshot)` call, so a device whose `deviceTypeId` changes to an unsupported/unknown family,
or whose payload stops validating, degrades in place instead of being silently mis-decoded or losing
its identity — while a family-valid update always recovers it.

Purpose: degrade-in-place is D-014's "preserve untrusted state" invariant applied to a whole-profile
failure rather than a single field. This is the last of the eight phase requirements and the only one with no domain HomeKit
service to visibly change yet (Phase 3 owns `SAFE-04`'s fault adapters and the per-service
`StatusActive`/`StatusFault` characteristics). What Phase 2 can and must deliver now is the complete,
reusable degradation MECHANISM — computed trust scopes, retained last-valid values, log-once
semantics, and an accessory that is never unregistered for this reason — ready for Phase 3's services
to read. `AccessoryInformation`'s own values are the one thing already published this phase that can
concretely demonstrate "keeps its last valid values" today.
Output: `basementGuardian.ts` computes and exposes degradation state; `platform.ts` no longer assumes
every `update()` call is against a still-valid family.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| A snapshot that fails family validation or resolves to a non-implemented family -> the accessory's retained last-valid state | The boundary that must never let an uninterpretable payload either overwrite good state or take the accessory itself down. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|------------------|
| T-02-07 | Tampering (state corruption via erased retained values) | `src/accessories/basementGuardian.ts` | medium | mitigate | `update()` never throws `HapStatusError` and never pushes an `Error` through `updateCharacteristic`; `AccessoryInformation`'s getters/characteristics keep their last-set values by construction (the degrade branch simply returns without touching them), proven by the "unchanged after degrading" test. |
| T-02-08 | Tampering (conflating a plugin-interpretation failure with a vendor-reported fault) | `src/accessories/basementGuardian.ts` | medium | mitigate | Every degraded `UntrustedScope` uses `reason: 'invalid'`, never a fault-specific reason, and no code in this phase sets `StatusFault`/`GENERAL_FAULT` at all (no domain service exists yet to carry it) — Phase 3's `SAFE-04` fault adapters are the only place that characteristic will ever be set, kept distinct by construction. |
