<objective>
The tracer for phase 05.1: carry the per-device silence answer that already exists in
`monitoringHealth.ts` all the way to HomeKit, and prove it end to end with two pumps, one silent and
one reporting.

Purpose: today one quiet pump makes every accessory on the account stop vouching, so a two-pump owner
is told the plugin cannot vouch for a pump that is reporting perfectly. The per-device answer is
already correct at its source — `silentDevices()` has answered per device since plan 05-14. The whole
defect is one flatten at `src/runtime/monitoringHealth.ts:212` and a signal path that carries a single
boolean. This plan removes the flatten and widens the path.

Output: `applyMonitoringHealth(context, account, byDevice)`, a runtime that resolves trust per device,
a harness mirror that changes with it, and a Cucumber scenario in which one pump goes untrusted while
its neighbour does not.
</objective>
